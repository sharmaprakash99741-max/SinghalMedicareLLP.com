About us

A family pharmacy, three decades strong.

Singhal Medicare LLP began as a neighbourhood pharmacy in Shikohabad and has served families across Firozabad district for more than 30 years. What started as a single counter of trusted medicines has grown into a full pharmacy-and-clinic — with over 10,000 products on shelf and eight specialist doctors sitting across the week.

We sit opposite Ahuja Hospital on Station Road, so patients coming from the hospital, the railway station, or the nearby mohallas can pick up a prescription, consult a specialist, and get computerised billing — all in one stop.

Our promise is simple: honest stock, honest prices, and advice you can trust. Always take medicines as advised by your doctor.

Frequently asked

Do you home-deliver medicines?

Please WhatsApp us your prescription and pin code — we will confirm delivery for your area.

Do I need an appointment to see a doctor?

For doctors with fixed sitting days, walk-ins during their hours are fine. For others, please call to confirm timing.

Which payments do you accept?

Cash, debit / credit card, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and UPI.

Are you open on Sunday?

Yes — every day, 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM IST.
